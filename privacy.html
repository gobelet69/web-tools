Privacy Policy

Swiss Army Knife of the Web is designed with privacy as a core principle.

  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account to use any of the tools.
  • Local Processing: Tools perform their processing locally in your web browser using modern Web APIs. Your files are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Local Storage: Your tool preferences (Favorites and Recently Used tools) are saved locally on your device using `localStorage`.
  • No Tracking: We do not include intrusive analytics or tracking scripts.
